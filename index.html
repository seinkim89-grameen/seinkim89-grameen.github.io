Health systems · Health economics · Global health

Sein Kim 김세인

Affiliated Researcher, Department of Global Health and Population, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health · Carolina Population Center, UNC

I am a health systems and health economics researcher working at the intersection of international development and public health. My research asks how evidence-based policies and programs can strengthen health systems and improve population health.

My work focuses on health financing, policy impact evaluation, and women's and children's health, with field-based studies across sub-Saharan Africa and Asia.

Research areas

Health financing

Out-of-pocket and informal payments, insurance and financial protection for maternal care, and the economic consequences of illness.

Policy impact evaluation

Causal evaluation and value-for-money modeling of programs, from school feeding to malaria testing and contraceptive supply chains.

Women's and children's health

Quality of antenatal, delivery and postnatal care, newborn risk management, family planning, and fathers' roles in parenting.
